<objective>
Author the two content guards for the O3 `ci.yml` work RED, before the `ci.yml` they assert on
exists, so that plan 11-06 can land the whole O3 instrumentation as the single GREEN commit.

Why the guards exist at all: the `o3-witness` job is a new CI job with no inferred Nx target behind
it. This project's own recorded trap is that `nx run-many` on a missing target exits 0, and by the
same logic a deleted CI JOB is a silently removable gate. `jobBlock(name)` in
`dogfood-cross-os.spec.ts` THROWS when the job key is absent, and that throw is the mechanism that
makes deletion loud.

Why TWO files rather than one. `dogfood-cross-os.spec.ts` strips every `#` line before matching, so
a comment lock placed there is vacuous by construction, and its own header says so.
`docs-same-os-claims.spec.ts` reads `ci.yml` RAW and is the only guard in the repo that can see a
comment at all. Presence and shape go to the first file; new rationale prose goes to the second.
Both carry the same rotation cost, so nothing is saved by choosing one.

Purpose: a gate that can be deleted without anything going red is not a gate, and the phase's whole
live-CI half rests on the witness job existing.
Output: two spec files with new, deliberately failing assertions.

**HEAD is deliberately RED at the end of this plan.** That is the recorded shape from plan `10-03`,
which authored its guards RED first and landed `ci.yml` as the single GREEN commit, with HEAD red
for two commits. Do not soften the assertions to make the suite green, and do not invent a
placeholder `ci.yml` job to satisfy them early.
</objective>

## The rotation cost is now being paid, deliberately

`nx.json` gives `test`, `typecheck`, `integration` and `lint` the unfiltered `default` named input
(`{projectRoot}/**/*`), and `build` explicitly excludes `src/**/*.spec.ts`. So editing a spec rotates
three of the four proof targets. Plan 11-04's sign-off is what authorises that, and O1 and O2 are
already banked in `11-EVIDENCE.md`. Do NOT attempt any local cross-OS cache measurement after this
plan lands: the mirror holds the pre-rotation hashes, so a MISS would be expected and would prove
nothing about cross-OS sharing.

to plans 11-02 and 11-03.
