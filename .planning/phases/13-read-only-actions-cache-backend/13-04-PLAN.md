<objective>
Pay the public-API cost of the knob honestly: enumerate `CACHE_READ_ONLY` in the single-sourced
consumer contract, document it on all three doc surfaces, and grow `docs/advanced.md`'s
backend-selection table from four outcomes to five.

Purpose: the contract cost is real and unavoidable — `EXPECTED_ENV_KNOBS` goes 8 to 9 and the knob
is reachable from the shipped package and the committed public bundle. The house rule is that
contract changes land as an obvious reviewable diff in an explicit list, never behind a
`toMatchSnapshot()` whose `-u` regen is easy to rubber-stamp. Upstream reframes the cost rather than
removing it: GitHub's own caching reference recommends the author-side equivalent ("switch to a
restore-only cache operation such as `actions/cache/restore`"), so a read-only opt-in is a
legitimate consumer capability with first-party precedent, not repo-local CI plumbing leaking into
the contract.

Output: one contract entry, one reviewed literal, three doc surfaces updated, and a spec clause that
keeps the outcome count honest.
</objective>
