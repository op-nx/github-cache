<objective>
Stop the publish mirror from re-enumerating prior runs' single-use CI seed entries, and make
the OBS-01 counts readable.

Purpose: 48 of the 63 `readMisses` on run `31281406708` are prior runs' seeds. The miss set is
self-perpetuating -- an entry that misses can never be mirrored, so it is never in the shard,
so it is retried forever. 38 of 87 shard assets are CI scratch.

Output: a structural seed filter (D1), a separable key shape for the last ambiguous seed family
(D2), a pre-restore shard-membership skip (D3), a split metric (D4), and a partial-miss guard
(D5).

Scope: stop the ACCRUAL. Per C4 the 48 existing stale entries are left to evict, and per C6
nothing is merged and no shard asset is deleted.
</objective>

Two things CONTEXT.md left to the planner's discretion are decided here so the executor does
not re-open them.

**The D2 marker word is `bead`.** It satisfies every stated constraint -- all `[a-f0-9]`,
leading hex LETTER, and neither a prefix of nor prefixed by `cafe` or `feed`, so the D1 filter
can tell the three families apart. It was chosen over the obvious `dead` for one reason:
`ci.yml` already ships the literal `deadbeef` as the consumer-smoke sidecar readiness probe, and
a `dead`-marked seed family would read as related to it under review while being entirely
unrelated. `bead` occurs ZERO times anywhere under `packages/` or `.github/` today (verified,
with the surrounding words as passing controls), so every occurrence after this task is one this
task authored.

**The D2 shape is the ci.yml `hash:` YAML change, NOT a TypeScript helper.** RESEARCH Q1
supersedes any framing to the contrary: the YAML route breaks zero specs, while a
`dogfoodSeedHash()` helper breaks the incidental `'run-1'` fixtures in `action/index.spec.ts`
and adds four more sites. D-14's stated reason for deriving in TS is that YAML cannot import
`CACHE_OS_VALUES`; a constant marker word has no such mapping, so that reason does not transfer.

**The D1 filter's admission rule is marker-prefix AND ends-with-this-run-id**, not a per-family
parse. It is family-agnostic (it covers `cafe<run>`, `bead<run>` and `feed<i><run>` with one
rule and needs no OS tuple), and its residual is in the SAFE direction: the only way it errs is
by ADMITTING an entry it could have skipped, which is exactly the status quo. It can never DROP
an entry, which is the direction C1 forbids.
